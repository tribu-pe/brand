# Brand use

The name **Tribu**, the seal symbol and the wordmark identify the Tribu project.

You may use the unmodified logo to refer to Tribu, its repositories, or compatibility with Tribu, and you may link to this repository or to [`tribu-pe/tribu`](https://github.com/tribu-pe/tribu).

Do not alter, recolour, stretch or redraw the logo, use it in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation without permission, or use the seal or the clay diamond as your own product mark.

The files are published for reference and for the uses above. No open licence is granted for the artwork; the rules in [guidelines.md](guidelines.md) apply. For any other use, open an issue in this repository.
